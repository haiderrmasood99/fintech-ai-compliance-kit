# SR 11-7-Style Model Card Template

## Model Purpose

What decision does the model support, and what decision does it not make?

## Intended Use

Who uses the output, in what workflow, with what human review?

## Data

Training data, validation data, exclusions, known gaps, lineage, and refresh cadence.

## Validation

Metrics, thresholds, backtesting, challenger model, calibration, and segment analysis.

## Limitations

Known failure modes, prohibited use cases, and escalation criteria.

## Monitoring

Performance drift, data drift, fairness checks, override rate, incident response.

## Governance

Owner, approver, review cadence, audit evidence location.
